## Abstract

This conference presentation compares RNN, GRU, and LSTM deep learning architectures for lithium-ion battery Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction, with each model's hyperparameters optimized to enable a fair comparison of predictive performance.

## Context

Presented as part of ongoing research at Sabancı University on battery degradation modeling, funded by the TÜBİTAK 1005 National New Ideas and Products Research Support Program (2025–2027).
