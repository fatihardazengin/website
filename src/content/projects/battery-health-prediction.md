## Overview

Ongoing research project at Sabancı University (2025–2027), developing deep learning models to predict the state-of-health (SOH) and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of lithium-ion batteries. The project is funded by the **TÜBİTAK 1005 National New Ideas and Products Research Support Program**.

## Approach

- RNN/GRU/LSTM architectures for time-series degradation modeling
- Bayesian optimization for hyperparameter tuning across model variants
- SHAP analysis to explain model predictions and identify key degradation drivers
- Battery-wise cross-validation to ensure generalization across cells

## Current Phase

The next phase involves collecting real battery cycling data from laboratory setups to extend model training and validation to real-world degradation measurements, as part of a real-time battery degradation data acquisition and prediction system.

Preliminary results were presented at the EV Charge Show 2025 in Istanbul, comparing RNN, GRU, and LSTM models for RUL prediction.
