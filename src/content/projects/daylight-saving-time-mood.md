## Overview

Research project (2021–2022) conducted as part of the TÜBİTAK EEEAG Project No. 121E013, under the supervision of Onur Varol at Sabancı University's Varol Lab.

## Approach

- Applied causality analysis to examine the effect of Daylight Saving Time transitions on human mood
- Developed interactive data visualizations to support the research findings
- Conducted as part of the TÜBİTAK STAR scholarship program
